### 一句话介绍

CEB 是一个把已验证 Founder CEB 文件转换为 PDF、Markdown、TXT 的原生工具和 Codex Skill，让长期停留在旧格式里的企业材料重新进入 AI 可处理的工作流。

### 这个项目为什么值得做

很多企业材料不是没有数字化，而是被旧格式挡在了新工作流之外。CEB 的痛点尤其具体：阅读器不稳定，导出流程不友好，批量处理更麻烦；更隐蔽的是，AI 解析失败时不一定会把失败说清楚。

“谁是专家”从一批家庭工作材料出发，把一次性手工导出变成可重复、可核验的批量转换。项目不承诺所有 CEB 都能处理，而是把已经验证的 Founder CEB v3 路径公开出来，支持 PDF、Markdown、TXT 和转换报告。

## English

### One-line description

CEB turns verified Founder CEB files into PDF, Markdown, and TXT so legacy enterprise material can enter an AI-readable workflow.

### Suggested GitHub About

**CEB | Founder CEB to PDF, Markdown and TXT for AI-readable enterprise workflows.**

### Why it exists

Enterprise digitization often leaves a stubborn format gap behind. Founder CEB files may still be present in internal archives, while the AI workflow expects PDF, Markdown, or plain text. Manual export is slow, difficult to audit, and hard to scale across a folder.

CEB packages a verified native conversion path as a reusable tool and Skill. It is deliberately honest about scope: unsupported versions and malformed structures fail clearly instead of being presented as successful conversions.
